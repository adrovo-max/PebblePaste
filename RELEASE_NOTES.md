# PebblePaste — copy, cross the screen, keep going

**Windows 0.3.2 · Intel Mac 0.2.3**

Use one keyboard and mouse across Windows PCs. Move the pointer across the
screen edge and continue on the next computer. Choose Portal, Ripple, or
Particle Jump effects to give the crossing a little motion, adjust their
intensity, or turn them off. A local preview lets you try the effects before
connecting another computer.

Copy text, links, files, and folders between Windows PCs and Intel Macs with
the shortcuts you already know. Pair each destination directly when using
several computers. No account or cloud file storage is needed for local sharing.

are Windows-only; they are not included in this Mac build.

## Get started

Save your work and exit PebblePaste from its system-tray or menu-bar menu before
updating. Closing the window may only hide it. Run the Windows installer, or open
the Mac disk image and move PebblePaste to Applications if prompted.

Join the same private network, compare all six pairing digits on both screens,
and enable sharing. For keyboard and mouse control, turn on **Keyboard & mouse**
on both Windows PCs. Use **Settings → Screen crossing effects** to preview or
adjust effects. Emergency return: **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Esc** on the controlling PC.

## Know the limits

Mouse sharing uses the primary display. Seamless dragging between computers
and carrying held keys or buttons across the edge are not supported. The
developer's cross-platform check is not certification for every device or
network. Physical two-PC keyboard/mouse takeover and interaction through the
effects have not been independently verified for this upload.

The Windows installer is unsigned. Do not disable Windows security. Keep
Gatekeeper enabled on Mac; checksums do not replace a publisher signature or
Apple notarization. Consult your administrator on managed computers.
